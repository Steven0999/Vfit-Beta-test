Privacy Centre
Your data and account controls

                
            

            

                
Device: VFIT saves a working copy on this phone so it can operate offline.

                
Cloud: Fitness, nutrition, readiness and AI Coach check-in data can sync to your Firebase account. Progress photos remain device-only.

                
Coach: A coach sees your synced data only after you approve their connection request.

                
Payments: When enabled, Stripe processes payment details; VFIT stores only membership status and Stripe identifiers.

            

            
            

                
                
            

            

                
Permanently delete account

                
Deletes your authentication account, cloud profile, device registrations, coach plans/conversations, beta reports and this account’s device-only progress photos. Export first if you want a copy.
